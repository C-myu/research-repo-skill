## English

This directory contains research papers on quantum hardware construction and implementation.

### Focus Areas

- **Superconducting Quantum Computers**: Transmon qubits, flux qubits, quantum processors
- **Trapped Ion Quantum Computers**: Ion trap technologies, coherent control, scaling
- **Photonic Quantum Computing**: Linear optical quantum computing, photonic qubits
- **Topological Quantum Computing**: Majorana fermions, topological protection
- **Quantum Control Systems**: Pulse shaping, error mitigation, calibration
- **Quantum Architecture**: Quantum processor design, interconnects, cryogenics

### Paper Organization

Papers are organized by technology platform and tagged with specific hardware implementations.

### Priority Tags

- `high`: Major hardware breakthroughs or scalability milestones
- `medium`: Significant improvements in qubit quality or control
- `low`: Incremental improvements or specialized hardware implementations

## 中文

本目录包含量子硬件构建和实现相关的研究论文。

### 重点领域

- **超导量子计算机**：Transmon量子比特、通量量子比特、量子处理器
- **离子阱量子计算机**：离子阱技术、相干控制、扩展性
- **光量子计算**：线性光学量子计算、光量子比特
- **拓扑量子计算**：马约拉纳费米子、拓扑保护
- **量子控制系统**：脉冲整形、误差缓解、校准
- **量子架构**：量子处理器设计、互连、低温技术

### 论文组织

论文按技术平台组织，并标记具体的硬件实现。

### 优先级标签

- `high`：重大硬件突破或可扩展性里程碑
- `medium`：量子比特质量或控制的重大改进
- `low`：渐进式改进或专门的硬件实现
