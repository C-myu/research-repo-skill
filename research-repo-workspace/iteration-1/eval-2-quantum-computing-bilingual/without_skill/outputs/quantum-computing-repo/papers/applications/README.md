## English

This directory contains research papers on practical applications of quantum computing.

### Focus Areas

- **Quantum Chemistry**: Molecular simulation, electronic structure, chemical reactions
- **Quantum Finance**: Portfolio optimization, option pricing, risk analysis
- **Quantum Optimization**: Combinatorial optimization, scheduling, logistics
- **Quantum Sensing**: Quantum metrology, sensors, measurement techniques
- **Quantum Networking**: Quantum communication, quantum internet, protocols
- **Practical Applications**: Real-world use cases, industry applications

### Paper Organization

Papers are organized by application domain and tagged with specific use cases.

### Priority Tags

- `high`: Demonstrated quantum advantage in practical applications
- `medium`: Novel applications or significant performance improvements
- `low`: Theoretical applications or incremental improvements

## 中文

本目录包含量子计算实际应用相关的研究论文。

### 重点领域

- **量子化学**：分子模拟、电子结构、化学反应
- **量子金融**：投资组合优化、期权定价、风险分析
- **量子优化**：组合优化、调度、物流
- **量子传感**：量子计量、传感器、测量技术
- **量子网络**：量子通信、量子互联网、协议
- **实际应用**：实际用例、工业应用

### 论文组织

论文按应用领域组织，并标记具体用例。

### 优先级标签

- `high`：在实际应用中展示量子优势
- `medium`：新颖应用或重大性能改进
- `low`：理论应用或渐进式改进
