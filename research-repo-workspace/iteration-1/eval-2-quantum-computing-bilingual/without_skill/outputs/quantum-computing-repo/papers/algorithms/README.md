## English

This directory contains research papers on quantum algorithms - the **highest priority** category in this repository.

### Focus Areas

- **Quantum Machine Learning**: QML algorithms, quantum neural networks, variational circuits
- **Quantum Optimization**: QAOA, quantum annealing, adiabatic quantum computing
- **Quantum Cryptography**: Post-quantum cryptography, quantum key distribution
- **Quantum Simulation**: Hamiltonian simulation, quantum chemistry algorithms
- **Quantum Error Correction**: Surface codes, fault-tolerant quantum computation
- **Algorithmic Complexity**: Quantum complexity theory, query complexity

### Paper Organization

Papers are organized by date added (newest first) and tagged with relevant sub-categories.

### Priority Tags

- `high`: Breakthrough algorithms with proven quantum advantage
- `medium`: Significant improvements to existing algorithms
- `low`: Incremental improvements or specialized topics

## 中文

本目录包含量子算法相关的研究论文 - 这是本仓库中**优先级最高**的分类。

### 重点领域

- **量子机器学习**：量子机器学习算法、量子神经网络、变分电路
- **量子优化**：QAOA、量子退火、绝热量子计算
- **量子密码学**：后量子密码学、量子密钥分发
- **量子模拟**：哈密顿量模拟、量子化学算法
- **量子纠错**：表面码、容错量子计算
- **算法复杂性**：量子复杂性理论、查询复杂性

### 论文组织

论文按添加日期组织（最新的在前），并标记相关的子分类。

### 优先级标签

- `high`：具有已证明量子优势的突破性算法
- `medium`：对现有算法的重大改进
- `low`：渐进式改进或专业主题
