# LLM Quantitative Finance Research Repository

A comprehensive research repository focused on Large Language Model applications in quantitative finance, financial analysis, risk management, and market sentiment analysis.

## Research Focus Areas

### 1. Financial Analysis and Prediction (Highest Priority)
- LLM-driven stock price prediction and forecasting
- Earnings call analysis and financial document processing
- Automated financial statement analysis and ratio extraction
- Market trend prediction using transformer models
- Financial time series forecasting with language models
- Quantitative trading signal generation

### 2. Risk Management and Portfolio Optimization
- LLM-based risk assessment and modeling
- Portfolio optimization with natural language constraints
- Credit risk analysis using language models
- Operational risk detection from textual reports
- Regulatory compliance monitoring with NLP
- Stress testing scenario generation

### 3. Market Sentiment Analysis
- Social media sentiment for market prediction
- News analysis and financial event extraction
- Earnings transcript sentiment scoring
- Central bank communication analysis
- Financial social network sentiment tracking
- Multi-modal sentiment fusion (text + market data)

## Key Financial Domain Concepts

### Financial Instruments
- **Equities**: Stocks, ETFs, indices (S&P 500, NASDAQ, Russell 2000)
- **Fixed Income**: Bonds, treasuries, corporate debt, municipal bonds
- **Derivatives**: Options, futures, swaps, structured products
- **Currencies**: Forex pairs, cryptocurrency pairs
- **Commodities**: Gold, oil, agricultural products

### Market Data Types
- **Price Data**: OHLCV (Open, High, Low, Close, Volume)
- **Fundamental Data**: Financial statements, ratios, metrics
- **Alternative Data**: Satellite imagery, credit card transactions, web scraping
- **Sentiment Data**: News, social media, analyst reports
- **Macro Data**: Economic indicators, central bank policies

### Risk Metrics
- **Market Risk**: VaR (Value at Risk), CVaR, beta, volatility
- **Credit Risk**: Default probability, credit spreads, CDS
- **Liquidity Risk**: Bid-ask spreads, trading volume, market depth
- **Operational Risk**: Model risk, fraud detection, compliance

### Portfolio Management
- **Optimization**: Mean-variance, robust optimization, Black-Litterman
- **Performance**: Sharpe ratio, Sortino ratio, alpha, information ratio
- **Rebalancing**: Dynamic allocation, tactical shifts
- **Constraints**: Sector limits, concentration limits, turnover constraints
